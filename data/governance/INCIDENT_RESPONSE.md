# Data Incident Response

1. Stop the affected acquisition, processing, sharing or publication path without destroying evidence.
2. Revoke exposed links/tokens and restrict affected storage.
3. Notify the project owner and designated data steward/supervisor through the approved private contact.
4. Record what data, participants, systems, time window and recipients may be affected without copying sensitive content into Git/issues.
5. Preserve safe audit metadata and hashes; do not download or redistribute exposed data for investigation unnecessarily.
6. Follow institutionally approved assessment, notification and remediation requirements; this template does not invent legal deadlines.
7. Rotate secrets, remove public copies where possible, rebuild affected manifests/artifacts and verify closure.
8. Document lessons and control changes in a non-sensitive decision record.
